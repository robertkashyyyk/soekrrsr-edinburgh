Rome

Fri 7 – Fri 15 May · eight days in the city, wide open. Both parties land at Fiumicino within the hour on the Friday morning — and it starts from there. A few seeds to draw out together.

✈ Landing Fri 7: Robert & Suzanne on the easyJet direct from Belfast (07:00 → 11:00); SO & Emily overnight from Buffalo via New York, into Fiumicino ~10:10 — so everyone’s through arrivals inside the hour. (Flights found, still to book.)
The ancient city

Colosseum & the Forum

The Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine Hill — a timed ticket, booked ahead, gets you all three.

Vatican City

St Peter's & the Museums

St Peter's Basilica, the Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel. Pre-book — the queues are legendary.

On foot

The baroque wander

Pantheon, Trevi Fountain, Piazza Navona and the Spanish Steps — all walkable in an afternoon.

Evenings

Trastevere & the food

Cobbled lanes and trattorias in Trastevere — cacio e pepe, carbonara, supplì, gelato. Maybe a food tour or a cooking class.

Art & green

Borghese Gallery & gardens

Bernini and Caravaggio in the Galleria Borghese (timed entry, book ahead), then the park — bikes, a rowboat, and the Pincio terrace over the city.

A day out

Out of the city

Eight days is plenty for a day trip — the fountains of Tivoli (Villa d'Este & Hadrian's Villa), or the ruins of Ostia Antica, a short train from town.

02

Rome → Barcelona

Sat 15 May · over to Barcelona for the ship, one night in town before we board. We're not here for a deep dive — just enough to say we saw it.

The hop

Fly Rome → Barcelona

Rome (FCO) → Barcelona (BCN), about 1h55 direct. Vueling 13:45 → 15:40 is the pick — lands mid-afternoon; their 17:45 is a touch cheaper if we want more of Rome's Saturday.

Do the sights, lightly

The open-top bus

Barcelona's a bit much to fall in love with in an evening — so a couple of hours on the hop-on open-top bus (Bus Turístic) does the job: Sagrada Família, Montjuïc, the waterfront, all from the top deck. Then tapas and an early night before boarding.

✈ Flights and the Barcelona night are still to book — a bed near the World Trade Center / cruise port makes Sunday's roll to the ship painless. See Still to plan below.
