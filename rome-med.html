Rome

Fri 7 – Fri 15 May · eight days in the city, wide open. Both parties land at Fiumicino within the hour on the Friday morning — and it starts from there. A few seeds to draw out together.

✈ Landing Fri 7: Robert & Suzanne on the easyJet direct from Belfast (07:00 → 11:00); SO & Emily overnight from Buffalo via New York, into Fiumicino ~10:10 — so everyone’s through arrivals inside the hour. (Flights found, still to book.)
The ancient city

Colosseum & the Forum

The Colosseum, Roman Forum and Palatine Hill — a timed ticket, booked ahead, gets you all three.

Vatican City

St Peter's & the Museums

St Peter's Basilica, the Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel. Pre-book — the queues are legendary.

On foot

The baroque wander

Pantheon, Trevi Fountain, Piazza Navona and the Spanish Steps — all walkable in an afternoon.

Evenings

Trastevere & the food

Cobbled lanes and trattorias in Trastevere — cacio e pepe, carbonara, supplì, gelato. Maybe a food tour.
