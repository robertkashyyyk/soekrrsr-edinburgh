Vintage, curated & old gold

In Sissi · walkable

Wander into these

Dia Xeiros — small family-run artisan shop, handmade and painted pieces, genuinely unlike the usual. Roots Stories — a newer concept store built around Greek and island-made work; Maria picks it herself. Sissi Leather — the effortless treasure hunt; browse slowly and see what turns up. Plus two family goldsmiths worth asking in: Fragiadakis Jewellery, forty years in 14k/18k/22k and the first place to ask about estate pieces and older stock, and the Sissi Jewellery Workshop, more workshop than boutique.

Worth the drive

Make a small trip of it

Yialenia Jewelry, Agios Nikolaos — designer-led handmade pieces, often Cretan in inspiration, and the owner will talk you through the story behind them. In Malia: Gallery Jewellery, an artisanal goldsmith's known for handcrafted traditional work, and Daedalus Jewellery Workshop tucked into the old village — a real working studio, and exactly the sort of place where a conversation leads to older gold, or to whoever locally has it.
